Acceptance of terms

By creating an account, signing in or using Ordrivo Courier as a guest, you agree to these terms and the Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, do not use the app. Mandatory rights under applicable law continue to apply.

02

Eligibility and your account

You must be at least 18, meet all local working-age requirements, be legally permitted to perform delivery work and hold any licence, insurance, registration or equipment required for your vehicle and location. Information you provide must be accurate and kept up to date.

03

Guest, registered and merchant-linked use

Guests, registered couriers who are not linked to a merchant, and registered couriers linked to a merchant may handle orders only when the app and backend mark them eligible for that order. Available actions differ by account relationship and order state. Platform-managed courier accounts are not currently active. You must not bypass these restrictions.

04

Account security

Keep your credentials, verification codes and device secure. Do not share or transfer your account. Notify support promptly if you suspect unauthorised use. You are responsible for activity performed through your account unless applicable law provides otherwise.

05

Accepting and completing deliveries

Only claim an order you are authorised and able to complete. Follow the in-app workflow, use accurate statuses, collect the correct order, protect food and goods, deliver to the stated destination and use any required QR or order-number verification. Report cancellation, no-show, safety or order issues honestly and promptly.

06

Location and availability

Foreground and, during an active delivery, background location may be required for navigation, progress tracking and proof of delivery operations. Granting device permission is your choice, but refusing or withdrawing it may prevent you from starting or continuing features that require live tracking. Do not falsify or manipulate your location.

07

Road safety and lawful conduct

Traffic rules and personal safety always take priority over app instructions. Do not hold or operate the phone while driving or riding; stop safely before interacting with the app. Treat customers, merchants and their property respectfully and comply with all applicable delivery, food-safety and transport rules.
