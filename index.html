Privacy Policy

How we use and protect your information

Your data is used to operate deliveries, protect accounts and provide support. Background location is used only during an active delivery.

01

Who is responsible

Ordrivo operates Ordrivo Courier and is responsible for the personal data described in this policy. Privacy questions and rights requests can be sent to privacy@ordrivo.com.

02

Data we collect

Depending on how you use the app, we process your name, email address, phone number, profile image, account and session information, device identifier and push token, vehicle details, identity, licence, insurance or registration documents, delivery and order status, support messages and attachments, and technical security information such as IP address and device details.

03

Guest and account modes

A guest session uses a device-linked guest identifier and may include a display name and vehicle type. Registered couriers may be unlinked or linked to a merchant. Merchant linkage and the current order state determine which delivery actions are available. Platform-managed courier accounts are not currently active.

04

Why we use data

We use data to create and secure accounts, verify eligibility, let you find, claim and complete permitted deliveries, provide navigation and live order progress, send operational notifications, answer support requests, prevent fraud and misuse, comply with legal duties and improve reliability. Depending on the activity, our legal basis is performance of a service, your consent or device permission, compliance with law, or our legitimate interests in safety and security.

05

Location and background tracking

Precise location is used for route guidance and active-delivery tracking. Background location starts only for an active delivery after you grant the required permission and may be shared with the relevant merchant, customer and Ordrivo systems to show progress. Tracking is stopped when the delivery reaches a final state or when the app can no longer run. You can withdraw location permission in your phone settings, but live tracking and some delivery functions will then stop.

06

Camera, photos and documents

Camera access is used to scan order QR codes. The app does not record audio. Photos or files are accessed only when you choose to upload a profile image, courier document or support attachment; selected files are sent to Ordrivo for that purpose.

07

Who receives data

We share only the information needed to operate a delivery with the relevant merchant and customer, such as courier identity, delivery status and live location during the active trip. We may also disclose information to service providers acting for us, authorities when legally required, or in connection with fraud, safety or legal claims. We do not sell personal data or use it for third-party advertising.

08

Third-party services

Google Maps processes map requests and location needed for routes. Expo's push service, Firebase Cloud Messaging on Android and Apple Push Notification service on iOS process a device push token and notification payload. Hosting, storage and security providers may process data on Ordrivo's instructions. These providers must protect data and may process it in other countries under appropriate safeguards.

09

Storage and retention

Authentication tokens are kept in encrypted device storage. We retain account, document, delivery, support and security records only as long as needed for the purposes above and any accounting, tax, fraud-prevention, dispute or legal obligations. Live location visibility ends with the active delivery; server retention is limited according to Ordrivo's retention schedule. Account deletion removes or anonymises data unless retention is legally required.

10

Security

We use encrypted connections, protected device storage, access controls and operational safeguards designed to protect personal data. No system is completely secure, so please keep your device and login details protected and contact us if you suspect unauthorised access.

11

Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability of your personal data, object to certain processing, and withdraw consent without affecting earlier lawful processing. You can delete your account from Profile > Account & data or contact us. You may also complain to your local data-protection authority.

12

Age requirement

Ordrivo Courier is a work-related service for couriers who are at least 18 and legally allowed to perform delivery work where they operate. It is not intended for children.

13

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app, our services or legal requirements change. We will update the date above and provide an additional notice where required. The version available in the app and on our public privacy page is the current version.
